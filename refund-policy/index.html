Refund Policy

Because Cash Web Studio provides custom digital services, payments are generally nonrefundable once work has begun.

Project deposits and initial payments reserve time on the project schedule and are nonrefundable after work begins. If a project is canceled before work begins, a refund may be issued at Cash Web Studio’s discretion, minus any completed administrative or planning work.

Payments for completed work, delivered services, consultation sessions, domain purchases, hosting, software, third-party services, and other nonrecoverable expenses are nonrefundable.

If Cash Web Studio is unable to complete the agreed-upon services, the client may receive a full or partial refund based on the amount of work completed.

Refund requests must be submitted in writing within 7 days of the applicable payment. Approved refunds will be returned to the original payment method.
