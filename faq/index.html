Website Projects

Planning and build

What kind of websites do you build?

We design and develop custom websites, landing pages, redesigns, portfolio sites, and service business websites. Projects can include strategy, design, development, easy content editing, foundational SEO, and post-launch support.

What platform do you use?

We build custom-coded websites for flexibility, performance, and design control. If you need frequent updates, we can add an easy editing area, often called a CMS or content management system, or recommend the best content setup for your project.

Updates

Updates and maintenance

Can I update my website after launch?

Yes. We can set up your website with a content management system, giving you a simple way to update text and images without touching code.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. Maintenance can include content updates, small design adjustments, troubleshooting, performance checks, and ongoing website improvements. We also offer guidance sessions if you want hands-on help with your current platform.

Visibility

Search and AI visibility

Do your websites include SEO setup?

Yes. Every website includes foundational SEO such as page titles, meta descriptions, heading structure, responsive design, performance-minded structure, and basic schema where appropriate.

Can you help my website show up in AI search results?

Yes. AEO, or answer engine optimization, helps your content answer common questions clearly. GEO, or generative engine optimization, helps your website make sense in AI-generated search experiences. These can be added when your site needs a deeper AI visibility strategy.
