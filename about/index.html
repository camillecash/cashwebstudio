About Cash Web Studio

Cash Web Studio designs and develops websites with care, clarity, and thoughtful structure so your online presence feels polished, useful, and easy to move through.

Camille Cash Founder

Studio Approach

Design direction with development judgment behind it

Cash Web Studio was created to help small businesses, founders, and service providers get websites that feel intentional from the first screen to the final form submission. The goal is not just to make a website look good, but to make it clear, easy to move through, and aligned with what visitors need to understand before they take the next step.

The studio is led by Camille Cash, a North Carolina-based web developer and designer who brings together technical depth, thoughtful design, and practical business sense. Her software engineering background spans healthcare, biotech, telecommunications, and enterprise software, giving her a systems-minded approach to every website she builds.

The work blends design direction with development judgment: clean layouts, practical user flows, responsive builds, thoughtful content structure, and a foundation that supports visibility online. Each project is shaped with care so the final site feels polished on the surface and practical underneath.

When it makes sense for the business, websites can also be set up with editable content areas so text and images are easier to update after launch. That way, the site can keep supporting the business as services, projects, and priorities evolve.

How We Work

A collaborative process without the technical overwhelm

The process is intentionally structured, but it still leaves room for conversation, refinement, and the practical decisions that show up once a website starts becoming real.

01

Clarify the goal

We define the audience, offers, content needs, search opportunities, and what the site needs to help people do.

02

Shape the experience

We map the pages, messaging, calls to action, SEO-friendly headings, and visual direction before the build gets heavy.

03

Build with context

We develop the site with responsive behavior, integrations, foundational SEO, metadata, performance, and future updates in mind.

04

Hand off with confidence

We review the live site, explain how editing works when a CMS is included, and support the next phase.
